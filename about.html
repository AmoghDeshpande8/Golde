Our Story

Golde was born in Brooklyn in 2017 from a 1-bedroom apartment.

I teamed up with my partner, Issey, to launch Golde with a single product (the Original Turmeric Latte Blend).

Since then, we've grown to a small-but-mighty team and have shared our superfood essentials with thousands of supporters.

– Trinity Mouzon Wofford

Co-founder & CEO
